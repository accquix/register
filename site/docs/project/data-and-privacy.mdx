## No data in this project

The repository contains code only: no register records, no dumps, no real names or identifiers in tests. Every
instance downloads the data from the official sources itself.

## Licences and attribution

| Source | Licence | Attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register of Legal Entities (RPO), Ministry of the Interior | CC BY 4.0 | "Register právnických osôb, Ministerstvo vnútra SR, CC BY 4.0" |
| Lists of the Financial Administration | CC0 per the exports page; the portal terms ask for attribution | "Zdroj údajov: Finančná správa SR" |

Every API response carries these texts in `attribution`. If you show the data to others, show them too.

**Never ingested:** the Financial Administration IBAN lists (`ds_dph_iban`, `ds_dph_oud`, CC BY-NC-ND: no commercial
use), and VIES (its terms forbid mirroring).

## You are the controller

About 80 % of RPO records are natural persons, mostly sole traders. Their business name and place of business are
personal data, even though the law publishes them. When you run an instance, you are the controller of that copy:

- decide and document your purpose and legal basis before offering name search to anyone;
- keep the API behind keys (the default);
- handle objections with `accquix-register suppress` (see [Operations](/self-hosting/operations));
- tell your users where the data comes from and as of when.

<Callout type="warning">
**The objection model is a protective default, not legal advice.** It keeps an objected subject out of name search
and keeps exact lookup by identifier. Whether that is the right answer to an objection, which objections you must
honour, and on which legal basis you process the data are your decisions as the controller. Assess them with your
own counsel for your use.
</Callout>

## What the register stores

| Stored and returned | Never stored |
| --- | --- |
| Business name, identifiers, place of business or seat, legal form, register entry, dates, status | Residence when it differs from the place of business, birth data, statutory bodies, stakeholders, representatives |

Natural persons without an IČO, known only by DIČ, are not in name search unless the operator turns it on
(`REGISTER_SEARCH_TAXPAYERS`), and then only by their full name. Either way the register returns only the DIČ, name
and municipality, only to authenticated callers, and never a person who objected. Whether a given use of that search
has a legal basis is the operator's decision. Logs contain no queries and no identifiers. The
software sends nothing anywhere except requests to the official sources: no telemetry.
